The rules, everywhere
1One main action, filled, bottom right. Named with a verb ("Save as JSON"), never "OK" or "Close".
2At most two helpers beside it, tonal, each with its key shown.
3Everything else in More, grouped and searchable, the same list as right-click.
4Settings are not actions. They live in a labelled Options box next to what they change.
5Results appear above the bar, carrying their own follow-up (Show in folder, Undo).
6The bar never moves. It stays pinned to the bottom however long the content is; Close is the corner ✕ with Esc.
