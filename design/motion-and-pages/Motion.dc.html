MOTION

Every movement has a cause

Six motions, used everywhere in the app. Reduce motion (Appearance or a mode) turns slides, springs and pulses off and keeps instant fades.

Page change

shared axis X · 420 ms · emphasized decelerate (0.05, 0.7, 0.1, 1) · ±28 px from the side of the tab you moved toward

Why: tells you which way you travelled in the tab strip.

Cards and rows arrive

rise 14 px + fade · 420 ms · gentle spring (back ease 0.35) · 45 ms stagger, capped at 12 items

Why: reading order follows the order things land.

Press

Start
scale 0.95 in 80 ms, back to 1 in 350 ms on a spring

Why: the button answers the finger before the work does.

Live status

Running
ring pulse · 1.8 s loop · only on things that are running right now

Why: alive things breathe; stopped things are still.

Open and close

Console
Done (7.9s)! For help, type "help"
height + fade · 360 ms · standard (0.2, 0, 0, 1)

Why: the panel grows out of the button that opened it.

Progress and hover

bar value glides 500 ms · card hover lifts 2 px and tints in 200 ms

Why: numbers that jump look broken; glides look measured.
